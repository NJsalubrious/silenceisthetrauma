Challenge your property Council Tax band

You can ask the Valuation Office Agency (VOA) to review your Council Tax band if you think it is wrong and you have been paying too much.

You must continue paying your Council Tax while the band is being reviewed.

Property Exemptions

Certain properties may be exempt from Council Tax or eligible for a severe reduction. This includes properties that are legally classified as derelict, uninhabitable, or undergoing major structural renovation.
