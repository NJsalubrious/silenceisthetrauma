Designing the Green City of Tomorrow.

We are committed to sustainable urban density, protecting our natural heritage, and ensuring Vancouver remains a global leader in eco-friendly infrastructure.

View Our 2030 Vision

Urban Canopy Protection

Our strict environmental regulations ensure that for every tree removed during commercial development, three more are planted. We rigorously audit all Tier 1 developers to ensure absolute compliance with our carbon-offset mandates.

Sustainable Zoning

By prioritizing high-density, low-impact residential zones, we are actively preventing urban sprawl from encroaching on our precious old-growth forests and provincial parklands.
