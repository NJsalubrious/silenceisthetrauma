The Bayesian Approach to Reality

Human memory is notoriously flawed. By teaching our students Bayes' Theorem, we equip them with the mathematical tools to evaluate the probability of their own perceptions. If an event is statistically impossible, the logical mind must conclude that the memory of that event is false.
