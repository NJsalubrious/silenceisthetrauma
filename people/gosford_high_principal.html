Welcome from the Principal

Welcome to Gosford High School.

My name is Phillip Miller, and it is my honour to step into the role of Principal at this proud school from Term 3. With years of leadership experience behind me — most recently at South Sydney High — I am eager to bring the same firm, fair, and forward-looking approach to the Gosford community.

Standards matter. Boundaries matter. Mutual respect between school and family is the foundation on which every successful child's education rests. I will work tirelessly with our wonderful staff to uphold the traditions of this school and to ensure every student is held to clear, consistent expectations.

My office is open to any parent or student engaging through proper channels. I look forward to meeting you.

Warm regards,
Phillip Miller
Principal
