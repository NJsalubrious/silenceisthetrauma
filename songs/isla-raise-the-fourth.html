Raise The Fourth

Frozen moment · rehearsed absence

Decoded Story

Raise the Fourth opens in dissonance. Isla isn't hiding behind metaphor. She's tracing a moment she once imagined might end her and singing about it as if it already has.

In another version, the ending floods. Here, it freezes. A still surface. Porcelain.
