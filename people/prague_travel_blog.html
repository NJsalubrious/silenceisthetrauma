Transit Zones.

Cheap trains, bad coffee, and long delays in Eastern Europe.

LOG: August 14th // Prague, Hlavní Nádraží

Another Day, Another Delayed Train.

Running on three hours of sleep and stale espresso.

Finally made it to Prague. The architecture is cool, I guess. Mostly I've just been hanging around the old town square trying to see the astronomical clock without getting crushed by the tour groups. The hostel Wi-Fi is garbage.

Things got weird yesterday, though. I was walking down an alley near the station trying to find a cheap place to eat, and I saw this guy who looked completely out of place. Intense eyes, moving way too fast. He bumped into some tourists and kept going, but he dropped a heavy, metal USB drive right near the gutter.

I picked it up. It looks encrypted. Definitely doesn't look like somebody's travel photos. I might plug it in at the internet cafe later if I get bored. Anyway, heading to Charles Bridge to kill time.
