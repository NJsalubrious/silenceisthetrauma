Protecting the builders of Sydney

A Message from Daz O'Malley

The United Construction Alliance stands firm against developer greed. We make sure that the men and women building the Apexian Obsidian Tower, and every other high-rise in this city, go home safely to their families.

Safety isn't a recommendation. It's a mandate. If an employer cuts corners on scaffolding, or tries to bypass proper concrete curing protocols, the UCA will shut the site down. We have zero tolerance for unsafe working environments.

Our recent negotiations with Apexian Developments have resulted in a historic "Harmony Agreement." This ensures that the Obsidian project will proceed without any unforeseen industrial action, provided the developers continue to respect the roster allocations we have mandated.
