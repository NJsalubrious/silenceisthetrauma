The Old Money Approach

Dunbar Capital was founded on the belief that real wealth is measured in acreage, not algorithms. For over a century, we have managed the pastoral interests and equity portfolios of select families who understand the value of long-term stability.

In recent years, the market has been flooded with new, volatile capital generated by tech conglomerates and speculative real estate ventures. These entities believe that with enough money, they can bypass regulatory frameworks and force out established landowners. They are severely mistaken.

We leverage our considerable capital and deep institutional relationships to ensure that the rule of law remains intact, and that our clients' holdings are protected from aggressive, extra-legal acquisitions.
