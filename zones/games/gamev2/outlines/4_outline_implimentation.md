### **Part 2: Orchestral Stems for *What You Don't See***

When Isla performs *What You Don't See* live with a full symphony orchestra, the instrumentation must perfectly reflect the "high class," hidden nature of her abuse. This isn't a traditional rock-meets-orchestra setup; it’s a surgical dissection of trauma.

If you were mixing this in your DAW, here is how you would balance the stems to create that specific psychological weight:

* **Stem 1: The "Perfumed" Strings (Violins & Violas)**
* *The Sound:* A continuous, incredibly tense, high-register tremolo. It never fully resolves into a comfortable chord.
* *The Narrative:* This represents her mother, who wore "perfume like armor" and unethnicized emotions. It is the sound of a "pressure system, with no show". It sounds beautiful but suffocating.


* **Stem 2: The Gaslight Woodwinds (Oboes & Flutes)**
* *The Sound:* Drifting, erratic, and overlapping melodies that seem to wander aimlessly without finding a root note.
* *The Narrative:* "Thoughts branched like rivers, none of them led out". This captures the feeling of being trapped in a maze, being told you are safe while being systematically dismantled.


* **Stem 3: The "Judge's Gavel" Percussion (Timpani & Industrial Scrapes)**
* *The Sound:* No standard drum kit. Only massive, sparse, booming impacts from the timpani, paired with the screech of bowed cymbals or scraped metal.
* *The Narrative:* It is the "sound of the floorboards cracking". It represents the heavy, unavoidable weight of the High Court judge's authority inside that house.


* **Stem 4: The Hinge (Isla's Vocal)**
* *The Sound:* Completely dry. Zero reverb, zero delay. Mixed uncomfortably close to the front.
* *The Narrative:* She refuses to be polished or blended into the mix. She is delivering the raw evidence. When she sings, "I’ll split the fracture, Tear the frame," her voice cuts through the orchestral illusion like a scalpel.


* **Stem 5: The Sub-Bass (The Drone)**
* *The Sound:* A persistent, 40Hz sub-bass drone that rattles the ribcage but is barely audible.
* *The Narrative:* The unspoken threat. The "silence" that learns to speak and finds the bones where you buried the weak.
