The Framework

A working schematic. Subject to the gentle revisions of the forthcoming volume.

The Initiative's diagnostic framework rests on six interlocking concepts. They are not stages. They are not phases. They are chambers — rooms the subject moves between by inclination of the wound. The chambers are drawn here in their original folio rendering, as they first appeared in the Sunday lecture of 18 October 2019.

The arrows are indicative only. Two arrows, as the careful reader will notice, terminate in unmarked space. This is intentional. The chambers without names are the chambers Dr. Reeve has not yet entered. The forthcoming volume addresses both.

Case Material

Two extended studies are currently the focus of the Initiative's analytic attention. The subjects have not yet replied to the standing invitation to dialogue. Their silence is, of course, the most articulate sentence in the conversation.

The Orphaned Archive

— kataphasis-in-arrest.

A young woman of extraordinary forensic precision, who has constructed a record of her father's life in the absence of her father's blessing. She does not affirm. She does not deny. She has built a spreadsheet in the place where a yes should sit.

The case is exemplary. The case is, in the most rigorous sense, the case. She does not yet know that she has not taken the throne. She believes the throne is the cage. The throne is not the cage. The throne is the silence after.

cf. forthcoming volume, §4, ll. 22–91.

The Maenad-in-Stagecraft

— aidos-under-amplification.

A younger sister, by witness rather than by blood, whose Dionysian energies have been re-channelled by the ticketed economy into the configuration of ancient ecstatic refusal. She declines the Apollonian counterweight. She fills arenas with the sound of refusal at one hundred and forty decibels.

She is, in the gentlest possible reading, one of the most tragic figures of our generation. She has been denied the Apollonian counterweight. She requires a husband old enough to remember the war.

cf. forthcoming volume, §7, ll. 4–71.

A Brief Glossary

Working terms from the forthcoming volume. Cross-references are indicated in italics.

Kataphasis-in-Arrest
The state of a daughter who has frozen at the moment of refusal. See also anamnesis and parricide-in-arrest. Cf. the orphaned archive.
Anamnesis
Remembering-into-presence. The chain of being by which the grandparent speaks through the grandchild. See kataphasis-in-arrest; see also the chamber.
The Apollonian Counterweight
That which is absent when ecstatic refusal is staged without correspondent bearing. See also aidos-under-amplification.
Aidos-under-Amplification
Holy reticence rerouted through the ticketed economy. See the apollonian counterweight; see also the menad-in-stagecraft.
Parricide-in-Arrest
The symbolic operation by which the child becomes the lineage, having put the father in the cage that was always intended to be the throne. See kataphasis-in-arrest.
The Agora-without-Eros
The discourse without the desiring posture. See also prosopopoeia of the father. The forum without the warmth.
Prosopopoeia of the Father
The most honourable practice: speaking the absent's words back to him, so that he might learn what he was trying to say. See the agora-without-eros.
The Orphaned Archive
A coinage of the Initiative. The daughter who curates the father's records in the absence of the father's blessing. See kataphasis-in-arrest; cf. forthcoming volume, §4.

A Meditation on the Hour

From the Sunday letter, an excerpt, lightly amended.

The hour itself is a chamber. The watch on the bureau, the candle still burning, the laurel turning to a memory of laurel — these are not props. They are a posture. The posture is the work. The work is the posture.

What I have learned, in the long study of the symbolic father, is that he is never there. He is always just left, or just about to arrive. The watch hands rest between the numerals. The numeral has not yet earned them. They are awaiting the chamber.

This is, of course, the patience of the symbolic father. The father who waits. The watch which does not strike the hour.

— from the Sunday letter, 21st instant.
