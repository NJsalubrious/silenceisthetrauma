Upholding the Integrity of Commerce

Welcome to the official portal for Judge Harrison Forde's chambers. With over two decades of experience on the bench, Judge Forde is committed to ensuring swift, fair, and decisive outcomes in complex corporate litigation.

This division handles high-stakes commercial disputes, antitrust allegations, and corporate malfeasance class actions. Judge Forde is renowned for his strict adherence to procedural law, ensuring that frivolous lawsuits do not unnecessarily burden the engines of global commerce.
